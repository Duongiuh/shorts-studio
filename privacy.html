Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 28, 2026

Shorts Studio ("the app") is a private tool that the owner of a YouTube channel runs on their own computer. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is handled.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

When the owner connects their Google account, the app requests these permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload) – to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's channel.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly) – to read the channel name and ID, and the status of videos the app uploaded.
  • View YouTube Analytics reports (yt-analytics.readonly) – to read performance statistics of the owner's own videos.

How the data is used

The data is used only to upload and schedule the owner's videos and to show the owner how their videos perform. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

  • All data – including the access token – is stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database outside that computer.
  • The data is never sold, shared with, or transferred to any third party.
  • The app does not use cookies or tracking technologies and does not collect data about viewers.

Retention and deletion

Statistics are kept locally for as long as the owner uses the app and are refreshed from YouTube periodically. Disconnecting the channel in the app deletes its stored token. The owner can also revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions; after that the app can no longer access any YouTube data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact information on the connected YouTube channel's About page.
