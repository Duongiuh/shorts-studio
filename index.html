Shorts Studio

A private, single-user tool that a YouTube creator runs on their own computer to produce, schedule and upload short videos to their own YouTube channel, and to read that channel's performance statistics.

What it does

  • Uploads videos created by the channel owner to the channel owner's YouTube channel, with title, description, tags and a scheduled publish time.
  • Reads the channel's own video statistics (views, watch time, likes) through the YouTube Analytics API so the owner can see which videos perform best.

Who can use it

Shorts Studio is not a public service and has no sign-up. It runs only on the owner's computer, and only the owner's Google account is connected to it.
